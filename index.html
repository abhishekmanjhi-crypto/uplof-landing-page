A note from the founder

Where does it break?

Most businesses I speak to are already doing something. There is a website, some search visibility, a form, maybe a CRM someone set up a while ago. The problem is rarely that nothing exists. It is that the pieces were built at different times and never joined up.

So I start by walking the journey the way a real enquiry does, from the first search to the moment someone on your team replies. Where it breaks is usually obvious once you look. That audit comes before any recommendation, because it decides what is actually worth building.

How I think about the work.

Traffic needs a next step.

It is easy to treat visits as the goal. But a business feels the difference at the point where someone decides to get in touch, and again at the point where someone replies. Those two moments are where most of the value sits, and they are usually the least looked after.

So I would rather fix the route first and grow the traffic into it. Smaller changes, checked against recorded events, tend to move the number of real enquiries more than a bigger campaign pointed at a page that cannot convert.
